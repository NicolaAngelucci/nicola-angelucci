Biography

Born in 1979 in Altino, in the province of Chieti, Italy, Nicola Angelucci is a drummer and composer.

He has won numerous awards and scholarships, including a scholarship to Columbia College Chicago in 2000, the Baronissi Jazz competition in 2000 and 2004, the Grand Prix du Public at the Tremplin Jazz d’Avignon in 2002 and the 26th Hoeilaart Jazz Contest in Belgium in 2004. He taught at the Orsara Jazz workshops in 2005 and 2006 and at the Eddie Lang Jazz Festival in 2012. His recording credits include approximately 70 albums as a sideman, four as a leader (including Identithree), and four as a co-leader.

His first album under his own name, The First One, was recorded largely in New York and released by Via Veneto Jazz in 2010. It features Sam Yahel and Roberto Tarenzi on organ and piano, Jeremy Pelt on trumpet, Paolo Recchia on saxophone, and Kengo Nakamura, Johannes Weidenmueller and Francesco Puglisi on bass.

His second album, Beyond the Drums (2013), was recorded with Recchia, Tarenzi and Puglisi, with whom Nicola had developed a close-knit quartet, and features special guest New York guitarist Peter Bernstein.

In 2021 he released his third album as a leader, Changes, consisting entirely of his own compositions, recorded with Olivia Trummer on piano and vocals (who also wrote the lyrics), Luca Bulgarelli on double bass and Gabriele Mirabassi on clarinet. In 2023 he co-led Dialogue’s Delight with pianist and vocalist Olivia Trummer (ADA Music Italy / Warner Music), a duo album featuring guest accordionist Luciano Biondini. In 2026 he released Identithree on GleAM Records, with pianist Enrico Zanisi and bassist Enzo Pietropaoli.

He has been a member of Fabrizio Bosso’s quartet since 2013 and works regularly with Bebo Ferra, Rosario Bonaccorso, Olivia Trummer, Dado Moroni, Max Ionata and Paolo Recchia.

As a sideman he has performed with leading jazz artists including Benny Golson (with whom he worked for seven years in Italy and abroad, from 2004 to 2011), Wynton Marsalis, Dee Dee Bridgewater, Mike Stern, Bob James, Steve Grossman, Sonny Fortune, Wess Anderson, Jeremy Pelt, Joel Frahm, Eddie Gomez, Andy Gravish, Jim Rotondi, Alex Sipiagin, Sam Yahel, Jerry Bergonzi, Aaron Goldberg, Larry Grenadier, Peter Bernstein, Dave Kikoski, John Hicks, George Garzone and Joey DeFrancesco.

His many recordings and collaborations also include Enrico Pieranunzi, Paolo Fresu, Enrico Rava, Max Ionata, Francesco Cafiso, Rosario Giuliani, Mario Biondi, Bob Mintzer, Alex Sipiagin, Peter Bernstein, Sam Yahel, Jeremy Pelt, Mike Stern, Rita Marcotulli, Fabrizio Bosso, Dado Moroni, Andrea Pozza, Perico Sambeat, Gabriele Mirabassi, Gegè Telesforo, Luca Mannutza, Nico Gori, Maurizio Rolli, Maurizio Giammarco, Stefania Tallini, Susanna Stivali and Javier Girotto.
